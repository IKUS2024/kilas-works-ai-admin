## Direction contract

THESIS: One customer workbench with Home always reachable and readable labels in the chosen language. Extend the existing white product rather than replacing its familiar workflows.

OWN-WORLD: White canvas, neutral warm rail, charcoal text, restrained orange actions, Manrope and existing outlined navigation icons. Language is a plain accessible select and Apply button.

STORY: Owners choose a product from Home, complete familiar AI, Video or Finance work, and return without entering the legacy workspace. Static controls translate; their own content stays intact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing rail and content column remain. Finance retains its greeting, scope controls and financial metrics. Home and language controls remain reachable on mobile; long balances and invoice filters fit the viewport.

FORM: Incumbent workbench extension, code-led. No new-world seed or replacement composition applies; preserve current structure and interactions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
